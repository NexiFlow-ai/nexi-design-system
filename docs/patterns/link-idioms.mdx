Three looks for three jobs: an *inline* link in running text (text colour, underlined, hover → secondary); a *row* link — the title cell of a table or row list (no underline, underline on hover, the row is the target); a *meta* link at 12 px secondary (*Open source chat*, *Why?*). A `<button>` that looks like a link is the inline look over a button with a 24 px target; a fourth tone, *quiet* (13 px · 500 · secondary, underline on hover), is the look of an action row (DS-7016). One class family `.nexus-link` (`.is-row` · `.is-meta`) and `.nexus-link-btn` (`.is-meta` · `.is-quiet`); nothing app-local.
