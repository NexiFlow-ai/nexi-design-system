When a list needs more than three filters, they leave the toolbar for a panel above (a wrapping row of labelled compact selects and chips, 12 px label over each) or beside the list; the toolbar keeps search and the primary action. Active filters show as removable chips with *Clear all*; a filtered-to-empty list says what it filtered. NexBI's `.nexus-bi-fbar` promoted; DS-7010 stays the ≤ 3 case.
