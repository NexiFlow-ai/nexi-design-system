From any app's Ask bar a question goes to chat-api as that app's Project; the answer appears in the Ask panel with its provenance line; a hand-off opens NexChat with the *asked from* chip and *Back to &lt;app>* returns. The conversation itself is governed by the Conversation Register, not here. The action card has two variants: *confirm* — verb · resource · values · Confirm · Change · Cancel, something runs after Confirm; *door* (`AskDoorCardView`) — title · one fact · a real link that opens a screen and runs nothing · *Not now*; states proposed · done (receipt line) · blocked (the reason, the door to the fix) · dismissed (folded to one line). A door is a route of a closed list; the kit follows nothing else. **The support door**: after a `not_on_screen · unreadable · no_route · no_destination` answer, a low-sufficiency `platform-docs` answer or `turn.state.stopped.credits`, the door variant reads *Ask NEXUS support →* with one fact (*your question travels only if you choose*) and opens the Support drawer (DS-5021); once per turn, never for a Visitor, never a model's pick.
