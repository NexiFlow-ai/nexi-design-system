Secondary actions under content are a quiet row of links, never a row of buttons: 13 px · 500 · secondary colour · underline on hover · a `·` between · baseline-aligned under the text, 8 above. *View plan · Pause · Change goal · Stop* under a goal card; *Connect Gmail · Not now* in the sentence that names the obstacle; the footer of an inspector section, a row's expansion, a result line, the recovery line of a state. A bordered button keeps three jobs only — the one primary commit of a surface, a confirmation's pair, the header or toolbar line's named actions. A destructive action in a row is the last link, named by its verb, never red — it confirms by name. One component renders the row (`ActionRow` of `LinkButton tone="quiet"`); nothing app-local.
