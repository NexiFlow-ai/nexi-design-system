**DS-6912** · `apps/care/src/components/inbox/SupportSignals.tsx#AutonomyBadge` · proposed

Two signals of the engine on a support conversation. The **autonomy badge** is a `Chip` with the level the engine chose — *Answer* (A0) · *Diagnose* (A1) · *Safe repair* (A2) · *Needs approval* (A3) · *Engineering* (A4) — and a `title` with its one-line meaning; a model never chooses it. The **red-line banner** (`role=status`) for a supervisor names the red line, *routed to the security queue* and *the assistant said its one line and nothing more*; for a `support_agent` the conversation is hidden and only *restricted — handled by a supervisor* is drawn, never the red line's name.
