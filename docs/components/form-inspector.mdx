**DS-3033** · `@nexus/ui/workbench#FormInspector` · adopted

Creation in the Inspector: *New …* opens the panel empty, the form fills it (fields 16 apart, DS-7014); focus lands on the first control. The footer is **one** primary at the toolbar size (32), right-aligned, **enabled throughout** — validation on submit, `error` above the actions — with Cancel as a link-button; never two bordered 36 px twins, never a disabled primary as the only clue. A sentence under the head rule (`lead`) is retired: what must be said before the first field is the head's subtitle; detail Inspectors keep no footer — the value is the control (DS-3034) or `EditBar`.
