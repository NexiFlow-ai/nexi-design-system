**DS-6608** · `apps/core/src/components/platform/OrganizationRequests.tsx#OrganizationRequestsPage · OrganizationRequestInspector` · proposed

Platform Operations › Organizations › **Requests**: the tab beside the registry (`LocalTabs`, DS-3023) under one header line and the operator-scope banner (DS-6806); the status scope as one `Segmented` line with counts (DS-3065 — *Pending* by default · *Approved* · *Declined* · *All*); a `DataTable` (DS-3030: requested · organization · name · size · country · language · status); the row opens the `Inspector` (DS-3031 · DS-7001) with four `InspectorSection`s — *Request* (name · work e-mail · role · phone) · *Organization* (name · size · country · language) · *Their words* · *Timeline* (submitted · confirmed · decided by id) — and, while pending, **Approve** (the one primary) and **Decline** in the footer. Each confirms in place with an `ActionPanel` (DS-3034 · DS-7004, never a modal): Approve pre-fills the organization name (2–80) and the region from the country, both editable; Decline asks for a reason kept with the record and never sent to the person. An approved row ends in *Approved · Open organization →* (the registry page); a declined one in *Declined* with its decider. Empty is *No requests yet* (or *none with this status*); denied is the 404 face of `OperatorGate` — the tab does not exist for anyone but a `provider_owner`. Not a kit duplicate: the composition is the row.
