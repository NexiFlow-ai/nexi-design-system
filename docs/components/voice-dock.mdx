**DS-4004** · `@nexus/app-shell#VoiceDock` · adopted

The shared Voice Dock: one component for every app — the compact line (mic · language · mic state · context · what is shared) and the expanded panel (transcript · understood as · the proposal, the choice between look-alikes, the context-missing ask or the frame's answer · the session's route and retention · the controls). Mute · Stop sharing · End & clear are three distinct controls; Text-only and Hands-free are toggles; the proposal card has one primary — *Confirm*, or the step-up door at L4 — and its change step is its own surface with *Re-propose*. The dock renders on synthetic scenes in the workshop and on `?voice_demo=<scene>` in an app; S2 replaces the scene with the real session. The controls are the kit's `Button` (`sm` · `ghost` · `destructive` · `primary`), the waves `@nexus/ui/voice#VoiceWaves`.
