**DS-3064** · `@nexus/ui/workbench#DatePicker` · adopted

The suite's calendar instead of the browser's `<input type="date">` popup (which cannot take the kit's colours, only its light/dark scheme): the Select trigger (DS-3011) with a calendar glyph, a month grid on the picker surface, the selected day as the primary pair (ink on paper), today a ring, other months muted, `Clear` · `Today` as links; `withTime` adds an hour and a minute Select. Values are the native wire shapes (`YYYY-MM-DD` · `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm`, local time).
