**DS-6802** · `apps/core/src/components/platform/Parts.tsx#WalletBucketsBar` · proposed

A wallet as four buckets: *available* and *reserved* as two `Stat`s, the active price-book version as a chip, the kit's `Bands` showing the share of each bucket (promotional · prepaid · contract commitment · adjustments), and one card per bucket with its remaining NXC and — for an expiring lot — the date it expires. A bucket with nothing in it is still listed. Read by the customer (Billing & credits) and the operator (an organization's NXC & Billing) alike; the customer never sees a ledger term. Not a kit duplicate: the kit has no wallet idiom.
