The branded mail of core-api as a story fixture: the envelope (to · subject), the wordmark, the 480 px white card with one `h1`, the body, at most one button, the button's target as text, the quiet footer, and the text alternative every mail carries — the same shape as the inline-styled HTML the server sends, drawn in tokens (no raw colour, no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`). `OrganizationRequestMail` is the four mails of in the request's language: *Confirm your request* (one button) · *New organization request — \{organization\}* to the provider owner (name · organization · size · country · language; never the goal, never the phone) · *Your organization is ready* (the organization bold in the sentence; one button *Proceed to your organization*) · *About your request* (declined — neutral, no button, no reason). S1 writes the Python twins; the stories are their UAT-1. Not a kit duplicate: the workshop had no mail idiom.
