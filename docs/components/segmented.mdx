**DS-3065** · `apps/core/src/components/common/Segmented.tsx#Segmented (proposed; promoted to @nexus/ui/workbench on adoption)` · proposed

The segmented single choice: two to six short options on one line, exactly one chosen, the chosen segment ink on paper. A native radio group — `fieldset` · `legend` · `label > input[type=radio]` per segment — so the keyboard path is the browser's (Tab into the group, arrows move the choice). `size="form"` is the 34 px control in a form; `size="toolbar"` the 32 px line above a list, with counts (the status scope of a queue). Not a duplicate: `RadioCard` (DS-3036) explains each option on its own row; `Select` (DS-3011) hides the options; a `Button` with `aria-pressed` is one toggle, not a choice. Proposed at S0 (08-UX §Design-system conformance); lives in NexCore until adopted.
