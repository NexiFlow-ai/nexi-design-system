**DS-6918** · `apps/tasks/src/components/inbox/QuestionReviewCard.tsx#QuestionReviewCard · QuestionReviewLive.tsx#QuestionReviewLive` · proposed

The reviewer's one screen for a `question_batch` approval: the Inbox card idiom with a table as its body, opened from the Inbox as a *resource page*. Head: *Review ⟨n⟩ questions · ⟨section⟩*, the source's classification chip, the requester and the time — on the page the one header line (`←` · title · chip · Ask) carries the title and chip and the card keeps the meta line (`chrome="page"`). Body: the row table (DS-3030) — *id · lang · category · question · gold facts · gold page · decision*. *question* is read-only text in the row's language (that is the job); *gold facts* is the count with a ✓ when the validator found every phrase verbatim, *negative* for a row without gold; *gold page* is the page id, a door to the page as ingested when the app has one; *decision* is a `keep | drop` segmented choice (DS-3065's radio group, default keep) with the reason `Select` (DS-3011) beside a drop — `not_answerable_from_page · ambiguous · duplicate · wrong_section · bad_gold`. Twins sit adjacent; a decision on one never moves the other. Foot: the validator's line (*Validator: 0 problems · 4 negatives · twins consistent*), then *Reject* and *Approve ⟨kept⟩ · Drop ⟨dropped⟩*; a drop without a reason is a row the batch cannot close on — Approve waits with *1 drop needs a reason* beside it, never a bare disabled button. Approve writes every row's pick (`decision.rows[]`) and closes; Reject declines the batch whole. The requester sees the picks read-only and *You cannot review your own batch*; an expired batch is the standard expired line; a decided batch shows the picks read-only with who and when and the tally. The text of a question lives only in the approval's payload: never in a pick, an event or a log.
