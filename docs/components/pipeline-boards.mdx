**DS-6105** · `apps/sales/src/components/screens/PipelineScreen.tsx#PipelineScreen` · adopted

**The board is VibeBan's** (amended 2026-10-12 — ADR-0066 A1 · (3); the owner: *"the pipeline must be a VibeBan capability, not a duplicate in the sales system"*): an opportunity is a VibeBan item of the pack's type on a *Pipeline* board (`nexus://tasks/project_template/sales-pipeline`), and the board — stages, cards, drag, limits, the sum — is NexTasks' own screen (DS-6201 card facts · rollups). NexSales draws no board: the screen is the projection sales-api composes as the person (`GET /sales/pipeline`) — one `DataTable` row per board (*board ↗ · key · open deals · pipeline sum*, `mixed` when the currencies disagree, *not yours to read* when the person may list but not read it), the row a door into NexTasks' Board tab. No board → `EmptyState` with the one door *New board in NexTasks*; NexTasks away → said as such. *Leads* and *Requests* stay two more views of the same screen (`LocalTabs`, DS-2006) until S2. The 2026-10-11 app-local board (columns · rails · drag · Shift+arrows · `⋯ Move to…`) is retired; its measures live in VibeBan's `.nexus-tasks-board`.
