**DS-4005** · `@nexus/app-shell#ShellDock (NotificationInbox)` · adopted

The bell's popover once a host feeds notifications (TBD-712 (a)): a head with the unread count and *Mark all read* as a link, the last eight items newest first — each a button: the kind's Lucide glyph, the subject by name, the Project, the relative time (the absolute as the tooltip), the unread dot — and a foot that offers the browser notification for a hidden tab (on · denied · *Turn on*). Identifiers and statuses only — a run's name, a Project's name, an artifact's title; never a message, a cell or a body. Opening an item marks it read and follows its link. The states are the shell's: loading, error with *Try again*, offline / reconnecting as a dashed strip over the list, empty; without a host feed the bell keeps the honest empty popover and never invents a count. The badge on the bell never grows past `99+`.

## Accessibility
