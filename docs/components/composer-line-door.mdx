**DS-6108** · `apps/sales/src/components/parts/NewLine.tsx#NewLine` · proposed

*New account* / *New contact* open the `FormInspector` (DS-7002) with **NexChat's composer in its one-line start mode** first. The person writes what they know — *"Name, ЄДРПОУ or a website…"* / *"Name, phone or e-mail…"* — and Enter or `↑` sorts it into the form **deterministically, without a model** (`lib/newLine.ts`: 8 · 10 · 12 digits → identifier, a domain → website, `+380…` / `0XX…` → phone, `@` → e-mail, the rest the name); the duplicate check answers on that line before the form is shown, so a twin is caught before a field is typed. *Start with an empty form* is the quiet exit (a link, never a second button). The composer is a `div` inside the Inspector's own form: `↑` is that form's submit, Enter asks it to submit; the footer's primary reads *Continue* on the line and *Create* on the form. One idiom for every record the suite creates ((1); NexForge's `/forge/new` and NexChat's *New flow* are the same door). Not a kit duplicate: the kit's composer is a chat turn, this is a record line; it is the promotion case for a `DS-3` *New … line* row once a third app uses it.
