**DS-6804** · `apps/core/src/components/platform/Parts.tsx#UsageTimeline` · proposed

Usage by `UsageServiceCode` over thirty days: one row per service and surface — the service's name and its code, the surface (Chat · VibeBan automation · NexBI · NexGraph …), the units, the NXC or the chip *counted, not priced* for a code the active book does not price (the owner's *count now, price later*), a `Sparkline` of the daily units, and for the operator the meter's source. A row any child of which was not metered says *partly unmetered*. A toggle replaces the sparklines with the plain table (the a11y alternative, DS-7008). A composition of the kit's `DataTable` and `Sparkline` — the vocabulary and the honesty chips are the row.
