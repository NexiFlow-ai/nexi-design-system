**DS-6705** · `apps/factory/src/components/parts/ReadinessPolicyEditor.tsx#ReadinessPolicyEditor` · proposed

The tenant's readiness policy: the gates in the order the engine runs them, a `required` switch per gate (DS-7003, labelled by the gate), the version line (*Policy v2 · saved 3 d ago by Olena · note*) and the versions before it. Editing is the `EditBar`'s (DS-3007): *Edit policy* wakes the switches and the order arrows and a note of ≤ 400 characters; *Save* writes **a new version** — the old one is never changed — and the line under the bar says what the service did (*Version 2 saved · 6 open orders re-evaluate*; the worker within `reeval_s`, the order pages follow their live region). A reader without `factory.policy.manage` sees the policy and a `Restriction` (DS-3009), never a disabled control; a refusal on save is the server's word on the bar. Not a kit duplicate: the kit's EditBar has no notion of a versioned, ordered rule set.
