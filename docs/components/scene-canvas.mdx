**DS-4010** · `@nexus/ui/scene#SceneCanvas · Scene3D · SemanticGlyph` · adopted

The one canvas every spatial surface of the suite draws on: a `SceneGraph` of objects (node · sticky · text · frame · shape · image, each with a semantic type and its glyph) and relations (nine kinds, each its own line and end), selection and multi-selection, a rubber band, drag · connect · place · edit-in-place, the ghost of a pending proposal and the objects it affects, a lock, a bound object's chip, another person's presence on what they hold, `fit`, a viewport, reduced motion. Colour is **content**: the eight named hues and their tints are `--nexus-data-<hue>` (DS-1005), tint · solid · outline the three fills; nothing on the canvas is a system meaning said in colour alone. The element's and the relation's menus float above the selection as render props (`selectionBar` · `relationBar`) — the canvas owns *where*, the host owns *what*. `Scene3D` is the same graph as solids through a real camera with the CAD gizmo and the presets; `SemanticGlyph` is the 26-type glyph set on `--nexus-icon-*`.
