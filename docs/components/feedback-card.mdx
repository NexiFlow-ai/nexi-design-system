**DS-6022** · `apps/forge/src/components/scene/FeedbackCard.tsx#FeedbackCard` · adopted

The one card over the composer that answers a spoken or typed turn on a scene (08 — one card, one primary, the `Why` fold beneath): a **proposal** to apply (the utterance quoted, the engine's level naming the weight — a level-3 change lists what it touches and asks to be read; *Apply* the primary, *Modify* · *Cancel* in an `ActionRow`), a **Which one?** with its options as buttons and their hints while the candidates glow on the canvas, **Done** with undo, an **answer** to a question, **not understood** with three things one could say instead (each sends itself), an **error** in the wording. Busy disables the secondaries and marks the primary. Until WP-9 this was `TurnCard`, a twin of the kit's chat turn card by name only; it is not that card — it carries no thread, no toolbar, no rating — and not `AskSurface` (DS-4002) either, because the scene's conversation is the scene's own, its receipt the canvas. Built on `ActionRow` (DS-7016), `Button` · `LinkButton` (DS-3001), `Chip` (DS-3003), `IconButton`, `WhyToggle`.
