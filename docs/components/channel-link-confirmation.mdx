**DS-6604** · `apps/core/src/components/channels/ChannelLinkScreen.tsx#ChannelLinkScreen · ChannelStepUpScreen.tsx#ChannelStepUpScreen` · adopted

The one page of the telegram-first ceremony after the five doors (DS-6602): a centred card in `BareShell` — *Link this Telegram account to your Nexi identity?* with two identity lines (`Facts`: the messenger's account · the signed-in person and organization), **Link** (the only primary) · *Not me*; then *Linked — return to Telegram* with the way back. The refusals are one sentence each with no second door: expired · already linked (either side, with a link to the Account page) · a WebView · error · offline. Nothing on the page is a secret: the token stays in the URL the person opened and is never printed. Not a kit duplicate: the kit has no confirmation card for an identity link. **The step-up face**: the same card at `/channels/telegram/step-up?token=` — *Verify it is you* (the only primary; sends the person through the provider again with `prompt=login` and back) · *Verifying…* (the button disabled) · *Verified — return to Telegram* · the refusals one sentence each: expired · not yours · one more sign-in (the session predates the request) · WebView · error · offline.
