Today is an instance of the **Overview page** (DS-7006, the NexCore cockpit — amended 2026-10-11 twice: the owner found three columns overloaded, then asked for the NexCore Overview idiom): a `wide` frame with the health line in the header (`StatusDot` + small age, as NexCore's *operational*); the **Sales pulse** frame (`PulseStrip` — Accounts ─ Opportunities ─ Quotes waiting ─ Follow-ups, each a door; `live` when the provider is connected; the foot line: planned · waiting · overdue · going quiet); three `Stat`s (planned today · open pipeline as money · going quiet, with overdue as the toned delta); then two `nexus-cc-two` rows of `Section`s — *Planned today* (`StatusList`: account · kind · contact · time, row → account with the Log composer open) · *Waiting on me* (the attention list: title · account · assignee · due, dot red when overdue, the task reference as the door to NexTasks) · *Going quiet* (`StatusList`: account · relationship · owner · *n d quiet*, amber ≥30 d, red ≥60 d) · *Recent* (the recent list: who · kind · account · outcome · ago). Only kit classes; no app-local stylesheet. The org-admin sees the pulse figures and the quiet empty state, never the lists.
