**DS-3020** · `@nexus/ui/workbench#PageFrame` · adopted

The content column. `layout="dense"` renders the one header line (DS-2005); `stacked` (eyebrow · title · lead · meta) is NexCore's and NexChat's and legacy elsewhere. `width` standard or wide. The stacked head gives way in one order (owner 2026-10-24): the Ask field shrinks 340 → 200 first; when the title (≥ 220) still cannot sit beside the actions, the actions wrap beneath it, right-aligned; the field grows back (340 · 440 focused) as room returns — the title never overlaps or folds.
