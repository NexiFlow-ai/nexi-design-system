`/core/channels`: one header line, then one `ChannelPolicyCard` per provider the contour has — the glyph · the name · the bot's public handle · the delivery state as `StatusDot` (receiving · stale · not receiving; never the word webhook) · two `Stat`s (connected people · messages today) · the *enabled* `Switch` (the change is the commit) · a `fieldset` the switch disables: the ceiling `Select` over the canonical ladder's reachable steps with the one sentence *Telegram relays messages through Telegram's servers* · what people may do (`CheckList`) · the inline-approval level `Select` (never · 1 · 2 · 3) · the actions kept to Nexi as removable `Chip`s. The empty state is the honest *not set up on this installation*; `policy_unavailable` is the fail-closed state said aloud. No secret is here. Not a kit duplicate: a policy card over a fixed set of controls.
