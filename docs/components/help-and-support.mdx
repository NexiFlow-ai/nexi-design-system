**DS-6909** · `apps/core/src/components/support/HelpSupportScreen.tsx#HelpSupportScreen` · proposed

NexCore › Help & support — the page every member of an organization can always find. One header line: the title, the *New request* button (opens the Support drawer, DS-5021). Two sections: *My organization's cases* — a `DataTable` of `SUP-n` · title · state · *expected reply by …* · last update, a row opens its public updates; *Support access* — the grant rows (DS-6910). The page shows the customer projection only: no internal note, no assignee, no engineering detail, no item key other than `SUP-n`. Not a kit duplicate: `PageFrame` + `Section` + `DataTable` with the support definitions as the row.
