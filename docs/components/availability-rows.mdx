**DS-6702** · `apps/factory/src/components/parts/AvailabilityRows.tsx#AvailabilityRows` · proposed

What the order needs and what the plant has, one `DataTable` (DS-7001) row per material: material · required · available · the lots that cover it (lot id · quantity · state — *released* · *quarantined* · *expired* — a `Chip` per lot) · the shortfall when the available quantity is short · the source chip (DS-6101) with the stock's verification time. A quarantined lot never counts as available; a stale stock read is said as such on the row, not hidden. Not a kit duplicate: the kit's table has no notion of a lot or of coverage. S2: the rows are the records door's projection as read — the quarantine story proves the lot is named and the coverage says what it holds back; the stale story proves the source chip says *stale*.
