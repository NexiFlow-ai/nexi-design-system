The sixth tab of Platform Operations › Organization, under `platform.support.read`: `Stat`s — open cases · SLA at risk · incidents affecting · last case; a short `DataTable` of the organization's `service_case`s (`SUP-n` · fingerprint component · state · opened · expected reply); two links — *Open customer project ↗* (the provider's VibeBan project, provider-tenant permission) · *Open support desk ↗* — never impersonation. The tab is absent for a role without `platform.support.read`; the Overview strip (*n incidents · m organizations*) is a `Stat` pair of the same read. No customer content, no person's name.
