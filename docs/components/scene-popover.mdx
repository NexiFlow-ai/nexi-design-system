**DS-4011** · `@nexus/ui/scene#ScenePop · ScenePopList · ScenePopItem · ScenePopRow · ScenePopSeg · ScenePopTitle` · adopted

The floating pop under a bar button of the scene canvas: a **menu** of items — plain (`menuitem`), one of several (`menuitemradio`, the chosen one bold), a toggle (`menuitemcheckbox`) — with the key at the right edge or the current value as a hint, a title over a group, a long list that scrolls (the 26 meanings); or a **dialog** of controls — a `radiogroup` row of swatches, the pill segment (tint · solid · outline). The bar owns which pop is open, Escape and the click outside; the pop owns its inside: a menu moves focus to its first (or checked) item and ↑ ↓ Home End walk the items, a dialog focuses its chosen radio. One surface, one radius (`--nexus-radius-lg`), one shadow (`--nexus-shadow-md`), one layer (`--nexus-layer-popover`). Replaces the eight raw `role="menu"` and three raw `role="dialog"` NexForge's bars drew (CONSISTENCY-AUDIT M1 · D1); it is not `Menu` (DS-3005) because it opens from a floating toolbar over a canvas, anchored to a selection, and holds controls a header menu never does.

## Accessibility
