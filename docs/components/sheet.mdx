**DS-3067** · `@nexus/ui/workbench#Sheet · SheetActions · SheetNote · SheetRow · SheetFootnote · SheetState` · adopted

The one modal frame of the suite: a backdrop on the overlay token, a 520 px card (`wide` 640) on the raised surface with the large radius and the medium shadow, a kicker (where the sheet belongs), a title, a body that scrolls on its own, and `SheetActions` at the foot — the one primary of the surface with its Cancel, or a lone *Close*. Inside it the ladder is `SheetState` (one quiet line), a thing with its actions is `SheetRow`, the legal line at the foot is `SheetFootnote`, and a destructive second step is `ActionPanel` with `danger` (DS-7004) in the same sheet — never a dialog over a dialog. The shell's Settings, Shortcuts and Connected accounts render through it; NexChat's `.nexus-modal` dialogs (New project · Move chat · Create from chat) adopt it in WP-3. A sheet is not the Inspector (DS-2007): it holds a device preference, an account, a choice that belongs to the person, not a resource of the page.
