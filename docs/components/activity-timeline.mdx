**DS-6104** · `apps/sales/src/components/screens/AccountScreen.tsx#AccountScreen` · adopted

The account's activity stream (amended 2026-10-11): the log composer (DS-6107) at the top, then **Next** — planned interactions and open follow-ups in one list, soonest first — and **History**, done interactions newest first. One row grammar for both: a glyph (open = planned or follow-up, filled = done) · when · kind or title · outcome chip · who · with whom · the summary · the next step with its follow-up door. The stream is the *Activity* view of the page; Deals · Quotes · Documents are its sibling views (`LocalTabs`, DS-2006). The source chip (DS-6101) on the page line says where these came from; when the source is unavailable the list stays, marked as the last copy. Not a kit duplicate: no kit export shows a dated sequence with a planned/done glyph.
