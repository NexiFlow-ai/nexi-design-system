DS-2005's header line applied to a room: **one line** — the glyph (`#`, a person's face, the wordmark for Nexi), the title, one quiet line of topic beside it, then at the right *Nexi is here* (a chip, when she is a member), the members chip (a door), search in the room, `⋯` (the host's menu — details · members · mute · leave, the last in the danger colour). A chat with a person is the same line with their face and their status; the chat with Nexi has no members and no leave; a thread's header is *Thread* and its close. No eyebrow, no lead, no tabs, no sentence about the product.
