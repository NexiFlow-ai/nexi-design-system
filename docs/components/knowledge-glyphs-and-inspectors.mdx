The vocabulary every NexGraph list, Inspector and stage line is built from. `Status` is a claim's lifecycle as a `StatusDot` tone **and** the word, with the *platform* scope said when the claim is the platform's; `TierChip` is the knowledge tier (Project · platform) on `Chip`; ** S2 amendment:** `VisibilityChip` is a version's visibility on `Chip` — *Shadow* (`warn`: a candidate under measurement that no reader opens — its row is a name and the chip, no content door, no diff) · *Superseded* (`muted`: the live twin a shadow replaced at activation); a live version carries no chip; the same word sits beside a shadow run's state on the Pipeline list and the run page (`run-mode-shadow`), never "shadow mode"; `MiniTable` is the kit's `DataTable` as it is. `ClaimInspector` is the human gate on one claim — the evidence passage, the model's proposal as a *proposed* fact with its model pin, **one primary** (*Verify*, opening the confirm `ActionPanel` that names the human scope and the closed reason codes) and *Return · Look again* as secondaries in an `ActionRow` (DS-7016); a question answered this sitting is *spent*; `MergeInspector` is the *same thing?* twin — two columns, the differing rows marked, *Same thing · Different* and the effects named before the POST. `GraphLegend` filters the stage by type with the counts; `StageStatus` is the stage's `role=status` line (*n of total shown · m lit · edges not drawn*); `TypeLegend` is the colour-cluster key; `ViewsMenu` and `SaveViewPanel` are a saved view's `Menu` (DS-3005) and its `ActionPanel` (name · what it keeps · replace). Built on `StatusDot` (DS-3004), `Chip` (DS-3003), `DataTable` (DS-3030), `Inspector` (DS-7010), `ActionRow`/ `ActionPanel` (DS-7016), `Menu` (DS-3005); not a kit duplicate — the kit has no claim, no tier, no stage.
