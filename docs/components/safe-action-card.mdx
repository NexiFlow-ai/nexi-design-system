**DS-6917** · `apps/care/src/components/widget/SafeActionCard.tsx#SafeActionCard` · proposed

The widget's card beside the assistant's line when the engine may run one catalogue action for the customer: the action's verb in the frame's language, the resource's short label, the one sentence *As your organization, within your credits. Nothing else changes.*, *Yes, ⟨verb⟩* and *Not now* — *Not now* is always present and costs nothing. A press writes the consent at the edge before any door is called; while it runs the buttons wait (`aria-busy`); the outcome is one calm `role=status` line — *Done — ⟨action⟩ completed.* · *⟨action⟩ did not complete. A person from our team will look into it.* · *⟨action⟩ could not run right now. A person from our team will confirm the next step with you.* · *A person from our team will confirm this with you.* (A3) — and the card is spent; a second *Yes* needs a new turn. The refusal code, a ref, a mechanism word (consent · grant · PDP) never reach the customer. One card per turn; never for a Visitor; never drawn on a red-line turn. uk = en (de falls back to en for the row's label).
