**DS-6806** · `apps/core/src/components/platform/Parts.tsx#OperatorScopeBanner` · proposed

One line at the top of every Platform Operations screen: *you are in the platform operator scope · metadata and money only · every action is recorded*, with the contour as a chip. It is the visible boundary of a scope that sees no customer content — the reason the person is here is said, once, on every screen, never in a tooltip. Not a kit duplicate: `Restriction` says what a person may not do; this says what a scope is.
