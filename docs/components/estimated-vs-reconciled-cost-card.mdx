**DS-6803** · `apps/core/src/components/platform/Parts.tsx#CostCard` · proposed

Cloud cost for one organization or the contour: *estimated* and *reconciled* as two `Stat`s each with its *as of*; one line saying when the cost export last landed (or that the contour has none); when the reconciled number is missing and an export is known, the card is in its **stale** state and says the export is late — it never derives a reconciled figure from the estimate; a BYOC organization says *customer-operated infrastructure — no NEXUS cost* and shows no number. The allocation drivers (rated units · sandbox seconds · storage bytes · active members with weight and share) open in a `details`. The companion `MoneyFive` shows the five money numbers (committed · consumed · owed · direct cost · gross margin) separately, with the basis of the cost named. Not a kit duplicate.
