The sidebar's *Recent* is the person's own conversations **across every Project and their Personal chats** ((a): opened or written in; `scope=mine`), newest first; each row carries where it lives as a trailing **mark**: the Project's accent dot — the same dot as its row under PROJECTS — or the person glyph for *Personal*, the words in the tooltip and in the link's accessible name; a hand-off chat's asked-from is the app's Lucide glyph in its accent (`AppIcon`, DS-1050) the same way — never the wide chip, which stays on the header. The title keeps the row. The Project's own list under its chevron stays the filter; the drawer's *All chats* (wide) keeps the words and a Project chip filter past 12 rows. The personal Project never appears under PROJECTS — only its chats. *Configure project* is the second item of the Project's `···` menu (after *New chat in this project*) for owners and editors, never a row under the chats, absent for others. *No chats yet.* is now true when shown. Not a kit duplicate: the sidebar row is NexChat's; the marks are the delta.
