**DS-5015** · `packages/ui/src/ask/ScriptedDialogue.tsx#ScriptedDialogue` · adopted

A conversation whose every line is closed content a human reviewed — a script of steps (text · chips · confirm) with validations and retry lines, en = uk, in the `nexus-scripted-dialogue-v1` format shipped as pack content (`packs/nexus-base/intake/*.json`) — rendered with the kit's thread and composer (DS-2008 line composer, the Ask turns): Nexi asks one question at a time; the person answers in the composer or taps a chip; a refused answer gets the step's retry line and the same question (never a form error, never a red field); the confirmation turn repeats what was said with *Create* · *Change*; *Change* offers the answered questions as chips and returns to the confirmation after the one answer; the host's `onSubmit` is the page's one write — a field the server refused (`422 code`) re-asks that step with its retry line; offline and other errors are one honest line and *Try again*, nothing created. No model reads an answer; the hook (`useScriptedDialogue`, a pure reducer) owns the state and never a fetch. Δ3: rendered in the one conversation frame (DS-5018 — the header says the intent, the turns are NexChat's own, the composer at the bottom); the speaker toggle and the browser's `speechSynthesis` are gone.

## Accessibility

the dialogue is a `section` named by the host; the thread is one `log`; one polite live region announces Nexi's newest line once; chips are real buttons in a `group` named by the question, in Tab order; the composer is the kit's (`role=search`, Enter sends, Shift+Enter a newline); the speaker is a toggle button with `aria-pressed`, disabled with a reason when the language has no voice; a refused answer is announced through the live region
