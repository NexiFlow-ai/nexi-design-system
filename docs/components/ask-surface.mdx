**DS-4002** · `@nexus/ui/ask#AskSurface` · adopted

The bar and its panel as one surface: the panel opens beneath the bar with the conversation for this screen, the answer turns rendered by NexChat's own turn card (DS-5001), the hand-off chip and *Back to ‹app›*. Its states are closed: empty · composing · streaming · answered · answered from the screen · not on the screen · unreadable · an action proposed (the proposal card with Confirm · Change · Cancel) · action done · error · denied · offline · no destination · no route. Each state has one copy class per language; nothing in the panel names a mechanism.
