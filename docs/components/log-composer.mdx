**DS-6107** · `apps/sales/src/components/parts/LogInteractionForm.tsx#LogInteractionForm` · proposed

The interaction is written **in the activity stream**, not in a side panel (proposed 2026-10-11, replacing the S0 FormInspector — the owner found the page overloaded with the panel open). Closed, it is one quiet line at the top of *Activity* — `+ Log a call, a meeting or a note…`. Open, it is one bordered block: a head (title · `×`), one row of four compact fields — kind · contact · when · outcome (`Field` + `Select compact`) — the summary (the only required field; its hint is the placeholder), an optional *Plan the next step* switch that reveals *next step · due*, and a footer where the save ladder (DS-6102) sits at the left and *Cancel · Save* (`Button size="toolbar"`) at the right. The header's *Log interaction* opens the same composer. With AI off the last line says so (08). Esc closes while nothing is in flight; the composer closes itself once the source confirmed the write. Built on `Field` · `Select` · `Switch` · `Button` · `IconButton` · `Hint`. Not a kit duplicate: `FormInspector` (DS-3032) is a panel; `EditBar` (DS-3033) has no fields.
