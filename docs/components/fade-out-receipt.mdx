After a terminal act — *Send* on a scripted dialogue — the thread fades over the fold's length (`--nexus-receipt-fold`, DS-1040) and what remains is one line, the receipt (*Your data is saved — thank you*), with *what happens next* under it; S1's saved page in place, no navigation. States `folding` → `folded`; held open while the closing clip is being read so the wave runs for it; reduced motion folds at once. The thread leaves the tree when folded — nothing stale remains. Used by DS-5015 when the script carries `lines.done`; 's intake hands over to DS-7017 instead.
