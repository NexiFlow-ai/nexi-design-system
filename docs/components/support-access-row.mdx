**DS-6910** · `apps/core/src/components/support/SupportAccessRow.tsx#SupportAccessRow` · proposed

One support access grant as the customer sees it: *support asked to read ⟨resource⟩ for ⟨duration⟩ — ⟨reason⟩*, the case `SUP-n`, who asked (first name), when. States — *pending* with *Approve* · *Refuse* · *waiting for NEXUS support's supervisor* (the second approval at `confidential` and above) · *active* with a text countdown, the reads list (resource · when · by first name) and *Revoke* · *expired* · *revoked* (by whom, when) · *refused*. A grant names one case and ≤ 10 resources; a new grant is a new row — nothing here re-activates. No mechanism word.
