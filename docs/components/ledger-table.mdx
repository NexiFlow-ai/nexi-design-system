**DS-6805** · `apps/core/src/components/platform/Parts.tsx#LedgerTable` · proposed

The append-only ledger of one wallet: one row per entry — when · the entry kind as a chip (grant · reserve · release · settle · expire · adjust · reverse) · the bucket · the signed NXC · the execution it belongs to · the reference (a reason code, the price-book version, or *compensates \{id\}* linking a correction to the row it compensates). Nothing is edited or deleted here: a correction is two visible rows. Operator-only (the customer's page shows buckets and usage, never the ledger). A composition of `DataTable` and `Chip` — the append-only idiom is the row.
