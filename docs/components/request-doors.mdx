**DS-6607** · `apps/core/src/components/auth/RequestDemo.tsx#RequestDemoScreen · RequestConfirmedScreen` · proposed

The two public pages of, anonymous, in the same centred card as the five doors (`AuthFrame`, DS-6602) with an eyebrow (*Request a demo*): `/request` — one column of `Field`s (DS-3010: your name · work e-mail · organization · your role) · the organization size as a `Segmented` choice (DS-3065) · country as a `Select` (DS-3011) · phone (optional) · the goal as a textarea with a counter (≤ 1 500) · the note *we will read your request and write back to this address* · **Send request →** (the one primary); then *Check your mail* with *Send again*. The refusals are one sentence each (`role=alert`): could not send · too many requests today · offline (the kit's state). `/request/confirm` — *We have your request, \{name\}* (the same sentence for an already-used link) · *This link no longer works* with one way back. A honeypot field off the page and off the tab order. Props-driven: the page holds the draft and validates what a browser can; the rung is the caller's. Not a kit duplicate: the kit has no public form card. No mechanism word; uk = en.
