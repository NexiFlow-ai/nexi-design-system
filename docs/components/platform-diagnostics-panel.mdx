**DS-6911** · `apps/care/src/components/inbox/PlatformDiagnostics.tsx#PlatformDiagnosticsPanel` · proposed

The provider desk's Inspector section for a `service_case` or a conversation whose Customer is an organization: the permanent line *metadata only — content needs the customer's grant*; `Facts` — organization · platform status · commercial status · component · error code · occurrences · failed runs 24 h · MAU · NXC available; the connections as name · provider · health `StatusDot` · last probe; the correlation refs as short read-only labels; the button *Request access to content* (opens the grant request form, DS-6916). Under the panel, from S4, the case's **grant strip** (DS-6916) — every grant with its state and *Open run* / *Open conversation* when `active`. The panel draws `SupportDiagnostics` and nothing else — a content field cannot be rendered because the contract has none. *Denied* for an agent without the platform support role; *empty* when the organization has no signals. From S5 the actions row carries *Escalate to engineering* (A4 — a human act): pressed once, the case's refs land as one `product_incident` through core-api and the row reads *Being worked on by engineering · ‹incident›* (`role=status`); a failure is one sentence and the button again; a case that already carries an `incident_ref` opens in the escalated state.
