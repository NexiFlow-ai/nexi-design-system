**DS-6916** · `apps/care/src/components/inbox/SupportGrant.tsx#GrantRequestForm · #GrantStrip · #GrantedRunView · #GrantedThreadView` · proposed

The provider desk's face of a `SupportAccessGrant`, under *Platform diagnostics*. **The request form** — opened by *Request access to content*: the case's `flow_run · thread` refs from the diagnostics as checkboxes (short labels, never content), one pasted reference, hours 1–24 (default 4), the reason the customer will read; *Send request* · *Cancel*; idle · validating · sent (`role=status`, names the case by its customer alias) · error (`role=alert`, the 422's words — *outside what can be asked*). **The grant strip** — the case's grants newest first with the state word (*Waiting for the customer* · *Needs a supervisor's confirmation* · *Active* · *Expired* · *Revoked by the customer* · *Refused by the customer*), hours and when asked, the reason, each scoped ref; *Open run* / *Open conversation* only on an `active` grant; nothing when the case has no grant. **The viewers** — render and forget: mounted on *Open*, fetched once with the person's own session under the customer's access, held in component state, unmounted on *Close*; the permanent line *shown while open — nothing here is kept, copied or added to a note*; a run is its Flow, status, start, failure code and steps; a conversation is its title and turns (*Customer* · *NEXUS*). A 404 reads as *No such run* / *No such conversation* — the same words whether the run never existed, lies outside the grant or the grant ended. No mechanism word (header · PDP · tenancy); no principal ref, organization id or work-item id is drawn.
