**DS-6109** · `apps/sales/src/components/parts/WorkRows.tsx#WorkRows` · adopted

**Deals, leads and requests are VibeBan's**: one row grammar for every VibeBan item NexSales lists on the account page (*Deals* view) and the Pipeline screen (*Leads* · *Requests* views) — *key · title · the kind's facts · stage · amount · the door*. The rows are read as the person (`tasks.items.list` through sales-api); the title, the stage's name and every field are tasks-api's words, and what the person may not read is simply not there. A deal shows its `amount` and close date; a lead its company and channel, and *became a deal* with the deal's door once converted; a request what it wants by when and, once a quote answers it, the quote's title as a link (`fields.quote_ref`, written by sales-api as the person) — or *Quote this* while none does and the person may quote (the one door into DS-7002 *New quote*, prefilled; the quote carries `request_ref`). `WorkDoorState` is the honest line above the lists when the door is shut: NexTasks away · no board yet · a board the person may list but not read (named, `role=status`, not an error). Built on `EmptyState` · `StatusDot` · `Chip` · `Button`; the list is the `.nexus-sales-rows` grammar of DS-6104. Not a kit duplicate: no kit export lists typed items of a sibling app with their doors and a per-kind action. Never a NexSales record — no table, no cache, no stage of its own.
