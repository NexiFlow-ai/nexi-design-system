**DS-6401** · `apps/map/src/components/workspace/labels.tsx#KindGlyph · StatusLine · MapControls · LayersPanel · ShortcutsSheet · Tooltip` · adopted

Everything that floats over NexMap's WebGL canvas and is DOM, not map: the kind glyphs (shipment · vessel · aircraft · hub by kind), the status line (cursor · zoom band · what is on screen · loading), the map controls (layers · zoom · reset north — `IconButton`s in a dock), the layers panel (a floating surface, DS-7015, with one switch per layer and the unplaced count as a door), the keyboard sheet (`?`) and the hover tooltip (feature · cell · zone · event · lane — one grammar, `role=presentation`, never focusable). Layers and dock tokens: `--nexus-z-dock` · `--nexus-z-popover`; the canvas itself is not a story (WebGL) — UAT-3 compares the running workspace to these. Not a kit duplicate: the kit has no map, no layers and no hover card.
