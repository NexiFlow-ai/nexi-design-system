**DS-6920** · `packages/ui/src/ask/RoomSidebar.tsx#RoomSidebar` · adopted

The left column of the Nexi desktop, on NexChat's sidebar idiom (DS-2001's 248 px column, the kit's rows): the brand row with Search (⌘K); the personal ↔ organization switch (DS-6923) only when the person has more than one workspace; **Nexi pinned first** — the wordmark for a face, her unread count, and when the person has not connected their Nexi account the row says *Not connected yet* and carries the one button; then *Rooms* (`#name` rows, the unread count as a number, Nexi's mark on the rooms she is in, muted rows quieter, the doors *Browse rooms* and *New room* in the group row) and *People* (a face with one letter — a picture only from the organization's directory, S8, never a relay URL — the unread count, *New message*); the host's dock at the foot (DS-2002). One highlight per column (the open row). Buzz's community rail, its pinned header, Starred, Forums, the relay card and the update card are not drawn — what the fork hides is hidden, not greyed. Presentational: the host owns the lists, the open row and every door.
