What NexBI owns: the block card (title · figure or chart · the *Data* twin · the trace door; `studio` adds the selection ring and the handles), the 12-column grid it sits on (drag · resize · keyboard, ~150 lines — no library, UX_PROPOSAL; a live region announces every move), the chart through `@nexus/ui`'s ECharts adapter and its accessible table twin (`SeriesTable`, the *Data* tab). `MetricStatusDot` (draft · proposed · certified · deprecated) and `CertifiedChip` are `StatusDot`/`Chip` with the metric vocabulary; `LinkRow` is the discovery row (DS-7001) with a meta column; `OpenInGraphLink` is the door to NexGraph. Not a kit duplicate: the kit has no grid, no block and no chart card.
