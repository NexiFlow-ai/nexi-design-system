**DS-3031** · `@nexus/ui/workbench#Inspector` · adopted

The right-hand detail panel: the head owns kind · title · one line of subtitle (13 px secondary, 4 under the title) / avatar · primary · `⋯` · close, 20 px inset, **16 above and below the head rule**; optional tabs; sections; body rows 4 apart; a sticky footer (rule · 12 · action · 12). 360–640 px by a keyboard separator (← → 16 px, Home / End, double-click resets), width persisted as `--inspector-width`; Esc closes and focus returns to the opener. Every vertical measure from DS-1011. **Push or over (owner 2026-10-24):** the panel pushes the page only while the page keeps 640 px beside it; when the split cannot hold both, the panel lays over the page's right edge (`data-layout="over"`: 1 px rule, `--nexus-shadow-md`, the drawer layer) and the page beneath keeps every column, its selection and its scroll (DS-7001) — never a table squeezed a column at a time, never a word a line. The splitter stays at the panel's edge in both. Under 720 px the panel is the whole screen.
