**DS-5009** · `apps/chat/src/components/quote/QuoteArtifactView.tsx#QuoteArtifactView` · adopted

A quoting document in the Canvas: the Request as five blocks A–E, every fact with its source; the Draft as lines that name their kind (reported · calculated · tariff · observed · TBC) and their basis in words; the reply beside its Ukrainian original with the same TBC marks. Its states are the Canvas's: empty · loading · a document that could not be read (named) · not found (the Project's 404, byte for byte the same for another legal entity's request) · offline (the last saved version with a banner). Margin, markup and the sale price are part of no view.
