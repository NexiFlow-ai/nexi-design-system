**DS-6706** · `apps/factory/src/components/parts/ExceptionFilingForm.tsx#ExceptionFilingForm` · proposed

A person files a production exception on the linked board, as themselves, from the order page: one inline `ActionPanel` (DS-3010) at the top of the order's stream — the kind (the board's closed vocabulary, suggested from the failed gate) · the severity · the gate it concerns (optional; a failed or unknown gate first) · a note of ≤ 400 characters. The answer is the item (*Filed as FAC-18 · in VibeBan ↗*) or the open item that already exists for this order and kind (the fingerprint rule — nothing is filed twice); the engine's own filings never pass through here. Refusals are the server's words: *not allowed* (the PEP), *the board is not linked* (`BOARD_NOT_LINKED` · `PROJECT_REQUIRED`), *VibeBan did not answer — nothing filed*. Not a kit duplicate: the kit's panel has no notion of a board item, a gate or a fingerprint.
