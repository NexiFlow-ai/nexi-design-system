**DS-3063** · `@nexus/ui/workbench#LinkButton · #ActionRow (ActionRow new, S3-B WP-0; `tone` · `href` on LinkButton)` · adopted

A `<button>` or `<a>` that reads as a link — the inline look (DS-7012) over a 24 px target — in three tones: `inline` (text colour, underlined), `meta` (12 px secondary), `quiet` (13 px · 500 · secondary, underline on hover: the goal card's actions). `ActionRow` lays its children on one baseline with an `aria-hidden` `·` between, wraps, and is the only container for secondary actions under content (DS-7016). Replaces the seven app-local link-button classes and the ≈ 40 `*-actions` containers of the audit.
