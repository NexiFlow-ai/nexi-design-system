**DS-6922** · `packages/ui/src/ask/SpinSettings.tsx#SpinSettingsSheet · #SettingsNexiAccount · #SettingsModels · #SettingsChoice` · adopted

The desktop's Settings as one sheet (DS-2010 — settings are sheets; the kit's `Sheet`, wide): the sections at the left under two group labels — *Personal*: Profile · Nexi account · Models; *App*: Appearance · Language · Notifications · Shortcuts · Updates — one panel at the right. Nothing of the surfaces the fork hides (Agents · Harnesses · Compute · Templates · Hosted communities · Archive) is drawn. **Nexi account** is the person's link: connected (who · which organization · since when) with *Disconnect*; not connected with the one button; linking with the trace and *Finish in the browser*; failed with *Try again* — no key, no console, no nsec. **Models** is the one place: *Auto* first, then the connections the person's organization holds in Synapse (name · provider; one that *needs a key in Nexi* is not pickable), *Manage in Nexi* opens NexCore's Synapse page — keys are never typed in Spin. *Profile* is the name and the key's backup row (the picture comes from the directory, S8). Appearance · Language · Notifications use the suite's one radio idiom (`SettingsChoice`). The suite's own `SettingsSheet` lives in `@nexus/app-shell`, which the fork does not vendor; this sheet draws the same idiom from the same tokens.
