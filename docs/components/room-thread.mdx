The log and the composer of a Spin room on the kit: the one scrolling log of room lines (DS-5022) with the day dividers and the *New* line, in the reading column as NexChat's thread scrolls, and the kit's line composer (DS-2008 `AskBar`: text · `+` when the host allows files · mic · ↑) pinned beneath, named *Message #room*. The `@` panel sits above the composer while the host has a query — a listbox with Nexi first when she is in the room, then the members; typing narrows it, a click or Enter puts the name in the draft. `mode="thread"` is the same log narrower: the root pinned first, the replies beneath, the composer *Reply in thread*. The ladder (DS-7006): populated · empty (*No messages yet — say hello.* and, when Nexi is a member, how to ask her) · loading (the trace and one line, no skeleton) · error (one sentence and *Try again*, no composer) · not a member (one sentence, nothing of the room — not-found sameness) · offline (the lines stay, the composer says so, nothing is sent — C-6). Presentational: the host (the desktop over its Nostr transport) owns every line, the draft and every door; nothing here fetches, signs or interprets; no decision is recorded here (rule 85 — chat-api's).
