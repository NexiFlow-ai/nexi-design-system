## DS-1061 — The brand mark and the wordmark

The platform is **Nexi**; its mark is `BrandMark` in `@nexus/ui` — a stroked N whose diagonal is a curve, with a dot at its centre, drawn in `currentColor` (the nexiflow.ai glyph). The wordmark is **one word whose capital is the glyph**: `BrandWordmark` draws the N at cap height on the baseline and `exi` in the row's bold type (16 px in the shell, 26 px on the doors) — never a mark beside a typed `Nexi`. Expanded shell brand row: the wordmark; NexCore follows it with the app name in tracked uppercase, NexChat ends the row with the Search icon. Collapsed rail: the bare glyph in a 32 px target. The unauthenticated doors (NexCore sign in · sign up · verify · welcome): the wordmark alone as the h1. Every application's favicon: the glyph white on a graphite rounded square (`src/app/icon.svg`). The letter N stays in the DOM, visually hidden, so assistive technology reads "Nexi". Applications keep their short names (NexChat · NexCore · NexBI · NexTasks · NexMap · NexCare · NexSales · NexForge · NexGraph); the wordmark is never translated and never written in capitals in copy (`Nexi`, not `NEXI`). Identifiers stay `nexus` (`@nexus/*`, `.nexus-*`, `nexus://`).
