## DS-1011 — Spacing

Ten steps — 2 · 4 · 6 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 48 px (`--nexus-space-1 … -10`): inside a control · icon to label · chip inset · the action gap · a field's gap · section inner · page head · between sections · page padding · the page's foot. Margins, paddings and gaps name a step, never a number; 10 and 14 do not exist (8/12, 12/16). A quiet control that must sit flush with its text uses `.is-flush`; off-screen text uses `.nexus-sr-only`, never a negative margin.

Tokens: `--nexus-space-1 … --nexus-space-10 (new) · --nexus-action-gap = space-4 · --nexus-page-pad · --nexus-section-gap · --nexus-inspector-pad`
