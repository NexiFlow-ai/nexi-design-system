## DS-1060 — Breakpoints and the reference viewport

Designs are judged at 1440 × 900. One breakpoint matters to the kit: under 720 px the Inspector becomes a sheet, the side navigation stacks above its content, and the header line wraps its chips and actions under the title. Below 480 px the shell is NexChat's phone layout.
