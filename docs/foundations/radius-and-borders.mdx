## DS-1020 — Radius

Seven radii — `xs` 4 (the inner corner: a chip, a swatch, a focus ring) · `control` 6 (the 34 px input, the 32 px toolbar control, a menu item) · `sm` 8 (button, card) · `md` 12 (panel, floating surface) · `lg` 16 (sheet) · `xl` 20 (dialog, launcher) · `pill` 999 (counter, status chip) — plus the composer's own 26 px. A `border-radius` is one of these names; `--nexus-radius` = `sm`.

Tokens: `--nexus-radius-xs · -control · -sm … -xl · -pill · --nexus-radius · --nexus-composer-radius`

## DS-1021 — Shell measures

The grid every app shares: 52 px rail, 52 px bar, 248 px sidebar (36 / 34 px rows), a 32 × 44 px collapse handle, 32 px rail buttons, 36 / 28 px button heights, 8 px action gap, 400 px drawer, 440 px confirm, 720 px composer, 800 px content. A screen that needs another measure proposes a token.

Tokens: `--nexus-sidebar-* · --nexus-bar-height · --nexus-btn-height* · --nexus-action-gap · --nexus-drawer-width · --nexus-confirm-width`
